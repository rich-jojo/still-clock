# STILL design direction

Brief: a sophisticated phone-as-object clock for physical Instagram filming, with arbitrary date/time down to the second and an explicit Start.

Reading this as: a camera-facing clock instrument for creators, in a typographic studio-object language. ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

- Signature: tightly set monumental condensed numerals, with the seconds as a small detached counter. Portrait stacks hour over minute; landscape lays them out horizontally.
- Chalk #f0eee8 with cobalt #243dcc is the default: blue printed matter on warm paper photographs as an intentional object, not a dashboard. Ink, blue and cherry are alternate full-surface photographic backgrounds, not decorative gradients.
- Ink #14181c, white #f7f5ef, cobalt #243dcc, cherry #9f173b. Each theme uses one foreground and one background plus a legible secondary tone.
- Barlow Condensed 600 gives stable, tightly packed tabular numerals and an identifiable silhouette. DM Sans 400 is for compact Latin metadata; Korean controls use the system font for legibility.
- Three compositions: Stack (poster scale), Line (classic readout), Split (physical card divisions). They share the same clock engine and restrained controls.
- Controls live outside the clock, then disappear entirely during capture. No watermark, logo, status, ad, hidden timer or prompt appears in capture.
- Real clock tick transitions only. No ambient loops, glow, fake chrome or decorative pictures. Reduced-motion disables all digit transitions.
- Settings is one native dialog, with large labelled fields and scrollable content. No external content is fetched at runtime.
- Begin paused at current local time on first load, not a made-up sample. Preserve the entered second across waiting, pause/resume and reset. Configuration persists locally; playback never resumes unexpectedly after reload.
- No accounts, no analytics, no permissions beyond optional browser fullscreen and screen wake lock. Do not claim a security audit or on-device Samsung test.
